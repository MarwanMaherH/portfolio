About

I build digital experiences from idea to reality.

Marwan works across the entire product journey — not just the part that shows up on screen. He designs interfaces, writes the backend that powers them, configures the infrastructure that keeps them running, and ships the result to real users.

He doesn't just build what a brief asks for. He looks for the detail nobody requested — the interaction, the transition, the small idea that makes a project feel considered rather than assembled.
